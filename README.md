# Insurance Corporation Financials: Visualization & Analysis

##  Project Overview
This project explores and visualizes the financial performance of an insurance corporation, analyzing premiums, claims, expenses, and profitability over multiple time horizons. Built entirely in Excel, this repository demonstrates foundational actuarial analysis, including the assessment of loss ratios, per-member cost scaling, and portfolio profitability. 

**Reference File:** `C_Project.xlsx`

## Skills & Tools Demonstrated
*   **Software:** Microsoft Excel
*   **Financial Metrics:** Loss Ratios, Premium/Claim Scaling, Profit Margins, Expense Analysis
*   **Data Visualization:** Bar Graphs, Pie Charts, Line & Scatter Plots (with trendlines & $R^2$), Histograms, Combination Charts
*   **Data Manipulation:** VLOOKUP/INDEX matching, dynamic referencing, historical data aggregation

---

##  Visualizations & Key Insights

The visualizations in `C_Project.xlsx` are broken down into five core tasks, each yielding distinct insights into the company's financial health:

### Task 1: Bar Graphs (Premiums vs. Claims Over Time)
*   **Objective:** Compare December Premiums and Claims over a 5-year period (2021–2025).
*   **Key Insight:** Both premiums and claims show a consistent, steady year-over-year growth trajectory. For instance, December premiums grew from approximately $16.1M in 2021 to $23.7M by 2024. The visual confirms that as the portfolio expanded, claim volumes scaled proportionally without erratic spikes.

### Task 2: Pie Charts (Financial Proportions)
*   **Objective:** Compare the percentage makeup of Claims, Expenses, and Profits between actuals in Dec 2021 and Dec 2026.
*   **Key Insight:** The corporation's profitability improved over this 5-year span. Profit margins expanded from **3.3%** in 2021 to **4.2%** in 2026. This was driven by a slight improvement in underwriting performance, as the proportion of revenue spent on claims dropped from 89.1% to 88.5%.

### Task 3: Line & Scatter Plots (Per-Member Trends)
*   **Objective:** Analyze the 10-year trend (2016–2025) of Premiums per Member and Claims per Member.
*   **Key Insight:** Both per-member metrics exhibit strong linear growth over the decade. By applying linear trendlines, the high $R^2$ values indicate highly predictable, stable increases in both per-capita costs and revenues, which is ideal for accurate future rate-setting and forecasting.

### Task 4: Histograms (Profitability Distribution)
*   **Objective:** Illustrate the distribution of monthly profits throughout 2026 using $800,000 bins.
*   **Key Insight:** While the majority of months experienced stable profits clustered between $0 and $1.6M, the distribution highlights key seasonal or catastrophic outliers. Notably, August resulted in a severe underwriting loss (-$1.19M), while November was an anomaly of high profitability ($3.28M). 

### Task 5: Combination Charts (Expenses vs. Loss Ratios)
*   **Objective:** Track monthly absolute Expenses (bar) against Loss Ratios (line) for 2026. 
*   **Key Insight:** The chart illustrates that operating expenses remained relatively flat and predictable (hovering around $1.8M to $1.9M monthly), but the loss ratios experienced significant volatility, ranging from a healthy 81.7% in January to a concerning 93.9% by May. This visualizes how claim frequency and severity drive month-to-month performance much more than fixed expenses.
